How it ranks

Every posting is scored 0–10 against your résumé: the kind of firm, whether the seat is investment, deal or markets work, and whether the title is at your level (two years in, CFA Level I passed, Level II candidate). Under 4 is left out. In the skyline, higher windows hold higher scores.
